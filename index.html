What We Offer

Considered, expert-led care

Every assessment and intervention programme is personally conducted by an experienced specialist and individually tailored to the child's unique strengths, developmental needs, family priorities, and cultural context, ensuring evidence-based, compassionate, and meaningful support.

Developmental Surveillance

Monitoring children's developmental milestones from infancy to identify delays before they compound.

Diagnostic Assessment

Comprehensive evaluation of emotional, behavioural, and developmental needs — including subtle, masked presentations.

Psychometric Evaluation for Workforce

Evidence-based cognitive, educational, personality, and developmental evaluations conducted by a registered test user.

Educational Assessment

Professional assessments that support educational planning, accommodations, and placement decisions.

Applied Behaviour Analysis (ABA)

Individualised intervention programmes rooted in behavioural science, adapted to the home's language and cultural context.

Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) Support

Assessment, intervention, family coaching, and school support for autism spectrum disorder (ASD) Support, focusing on early years, and presentations.

Professional Certification Training

Training pathways for behavioural analysts, educators, therapists, and support professionals seeking International Behaviour Analyst Board (IBAO) and Qualified Applied Behaviour Analysis (QABA) credentials.

School Health Screening

Conducting evidence-based developmental and educational screenings within schools to facilitate the early identification of developmental, behavioural, and learning needs, enabling timely intervention and appropriate support before more intensive referrals become necessary.

Workforce Capacity

Evidence-based workforce development and neurodiversity inclusion programmes for healthcare providers and organisations.

Meet Helen

Helen Obiageli Oshikoya PhD (Hon.), LLB, BL, MA (Social Sciences), IBA, QBA, CEA, RQTU, SAAE  ·  Founder & CEO, Nobelova Gradani

Helen Obiageli Oshikoya PhD (Hon.)

Helen Obiageli Oshikoya is a Nigerian mental health advocate, neurodevelopmental specialist, educational assessor, and behaviour analyst who has played a significant role in advancing developmental screening, autism awareness, and evidence-based intervention services in Nigeria. She is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Nobelova Gradani PsychoEducational Services, an organisation dedicated to improving access to early identification, assessment, intervention, and support for children and adults with neurodevelopmental and mental health needs.

Originally trained as a legal practitioner and a member of the Nigerian Bar Association since 1991, Helen later transitioned into the field of mental health and special education after recognising the urgent need for specialised services for children with developmental challenges. She earned a Master's degree in Social Science from the United Kingdom, with a focus on child development, learning assessments, and mental health, and subsequently obtained several international professional qualifications in autism, educational assessment, and behaviour analysis.

Throughout her career, Helen has championed the rights of children with disabilities and has been a leading advocate for developmental surveillance, early diagnosis, and evidence-based intervention in Nigeria. Through Nobelova Gradani, she has worked closely with hospitals, schools, families, and government agencies to promote routine developmental screening, educational assessments, inclusive education, and Applied Behaviour Analysis (ABA). Her work has contributed significantly to increasing awareness of neurodevelopmental disorders and improving access to professional services for children across the country.

Helen is recognised internationally for her expertise and professional leadership. She is a Qualified Autism Services Practitioner–Supervisor (QASP-S), a Certified Autism Specialist (CAS), a Chartered Educational Assessor, and an International Behaviour Analyst. She has also served as an international trainer in behaviour analysis and has contributed to the development of professional standards and workforce training within Africa's growing neurodevelopmental sector.

Her professional memberships include the Nigerian Psychological Association, the British Psychological Society, and the American Psychological Association. She has been actively involved in developing educational policies, supporting inclusive learning environments, and mentoring professionals in special education, behaviour analysis, and developmental assessment.

Helen's contributions have received national and international recognition. She was honoured as one of the BusinessDay Women Champions in 2020 for her work in mental healthcare and advocacy for children with special educational needs. Her vision continues to focus on ensuring that every child has access to early identification, quality intervention, and inclusive educational opportunities regardless of disability or socioeconomic background.

Driven by the belief that every child deserves the opportunity to reach their full potential, Helen Obiageli Oshikoya remains one of Nigeria's leading voices in developmental screening, autism services, educational assessment, and the advancement of evidence-based practices in mental health and special education.
